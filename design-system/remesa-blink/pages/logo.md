# Logo — holatia.app

Master: `design-system/remesa-blink/MASTER.md`

Consumer mark for **holatia** (hola + TIA). Remesa Blink remains the rail name; holatia is what the family sees.

## Tokens (do not override Master)

- Nopal `#2d5016` stamp
- Papel `#f5f0e8` bubble
- Inner symbol from **TIA logos system**: Georgia **T** `#1A4A2E` + gold dot `#C9A84C`
- Tierra `#2c2416` on **hola**; nopal on **tia**; gold underline `#C9A84C`
- Display font: Palatino. No navy, no purple, no official Mexican coat of arms.
- Source kit: `frontend/public/brand/tia-system/` (copy of Downloads/TIA logos system). Do not swap the holatia wordmark for the horizontal TIA lockup.

## Variants

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `frontend/components/HolatiaMark.tsx` | Nav, 40px UI |

Clear space = height of the stamp. Min mark 24px digital / 10mm print.

## Anti-patterns

- Solana S, USDC coin, eagle, photoreal nopal
- Wordmark in IBM Plex (UI only)
- Emoji as the mark
